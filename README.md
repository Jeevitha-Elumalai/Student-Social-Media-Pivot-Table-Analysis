📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing a Student Social Media Dataset using Microsoft Excel Pivot Tables. The objective is to explore students' social media usage patterns and understand how social media impacts their academic performance, sleep hours, mental health, addiction scores, and relationship status.

The analysis uses Pivot Tables to summarize, compare, and interpret student data to identify meaningful patterns and insights.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze the gender distribution of students.

Understand the distribution of students across academic levels.

Identify the most commonly used social media platforms.

Explore social media addiction scores among students.

Analyze the impact of social media on academic performance.

Compare average addiction scores between male and female students.

Identify average sleep hours across academic levels.

Analyze students' relationship status.

Compare average daily social media usage hours across platforms.

Understand the relationship between addiction scores and mental health scores.

📈 Key Project Insights

Instagram is the most widely used social media platform.

Female students have a higher average addiction score than male students.
